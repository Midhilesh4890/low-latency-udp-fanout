# How to submit your solution

🇷🇺 [Русская версия](how_to_submit_solution.md)

Solutions are submitted through your own personal repository. You can copy the starter code from the task repository into it. Only you and the organizer will see it — other participants have no access.

To submit your solution:

1. Open the task project and click **Fork** — this creates a copy of the project in your personal namespace.
2. Make sure it is **private**. This is critical — otherwise other participants will be able to see your solution.
3. Work in your own repository: clone it, implement your solution there, and push to the remote.
4. What your repository should contain (if you have more to add beyond this — great):
   - The solution code
   - An ipython notebook with plots, analysis, and conclusions
   - A document with your ideas, decisions, and an analysis of the constraints you did and did not account for (this can live in the same ipython notebook)
   - If you'd like to be considered for our open positions, you're welcome to attach your CV to the solution
5. Grant the organizer access: in your repository go to **Manage → Members → Invite member** and add the user **@spectral** with the **Reporter** role.
6. Your submission is your last commit before the deadline — 30 August 2026, 23:59 MSK — provided the organizer has been granted access. Make sure everything is pushed and access is granted by that time.

## Important — confidentiality

- Keep the repository **Private**; do not switch it to Public/Internal
- Do not submit via a merge request to the original project — that is visible to other participants
- Do not give access to other participants and do not publish your solution anywhere else
- Do not attempt to access solutions from other participants

Violating these rules may result in disqualification.
